## Project Summary

IlliniSpaces is a platform designed to help students and organizations efficiently locate and claim available meeting rooms on campus. By integrating university scheduling data from courses.illinois.edu and one.illinois.edu, the platform provides real-time insights into open spaces. Users can filter rooms based on availability, capacity, and distance, streamlining the booking process. Users can also make informal reservations on our platform, allowing for user coordination based on room availability. Additionally, an interactive campus map will enhance visualization by displaying room availability through color-coded indicators, with potential expansions for route mapping and calendar-based scheduling. With a centralized and accessible interface, IlliniSpaces optimizes campus space usage and enhances the student experience at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.
